# Finza — Finance Tracker

A personal finance tracker that makes your money feel understandable. Log your spending, let the app sort your transactions automatically, keep an eye on monthly budgets, and get gentle insight cards about your habits — no charts for chart's sake, no jargon.

## What it does

- **Dashboard** — your balance, this month's spend and income, savings rate, a weekly spend pulse, top categories, and recent activity.
- **Transactions** — the full list with search, type filter, sorting, automatic categorization, and add/edit/delete.
- **Budgets** — set monthly limits per category and view progress at a glance.
- **Insights** — matter-of-fact notes on your spending rhythms (positive / neutral / warning).
- **Settings** — currency, region, and your data (export / reset).

## Look and feel

A calm, dark design with cyan and violet aurora accents. Money is shown in Moroccan Dirham (MAD), formatted clearly and consistently. A demo mode lets you explore everything without signing up — it runs fully in the browser with sample data.
